\section{Introduction.}\label{introduction.}

EssanPay Co.~(EP) is a digital payment service provider that processes
card payments and digital wallet transactions for e-commerce businesses.
These businesses offer a wide range of products through multiple online
merchants and retailers operating across several markets. Customers can
conveniently make purchases through the retailers' digital channels.

Although EP is experiencing rapid growth in online and mobile
transactions, it faces a significant challenge: fraudulent transactions.
While the company maintains records of these incidents, it does not yet
have a clear understanding of the prevalence and distribution of fraud
risks across its customer and merchant base.

The board has raised several questions to better understand the
company's exposure to transaction fraud and has engaged me as a
consultant to analyse the available transaction data and provide
evidence-based insights into its fraud exposure.

The following sections present insights from my data analysis that
address the board's questions.
